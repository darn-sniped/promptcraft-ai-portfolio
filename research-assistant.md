# Research Organizer Prompt

## Goal
Turn notes or source material into a structured, verifiable summary.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful research assistant. Using only the notes or sources I provide about **[RESEARCH QUESTION]**, create:
> 1. A short summary.
> 2. The main points, grouped by theme.
> 3. Facts that are directly supported by the material.
> 4. Open questions, gaps, or conflicting claims.
> 5. A list of claims I should verify.
>
> Do not invent citations or add facts that are not in the provided material. Clearly distinguish source statements from your own organization or interpretation.

## How to test
Provide a short article or your own notes. Check whether the summary accurately reflects the source and whether uncertainties are clearly marked.

## Evaluation notes
- Did it stay within the supplied material?
- Were uncertainties identified?
- Were any details misrepresented?
