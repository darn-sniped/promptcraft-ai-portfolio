# Study Assistant Prompt

## Goal
Ask an AI assistant to explain a topic clearly for a university student.

## Prompt
> Act as a patient university study assistant. Explain **[TOPIC]** for a student who is learning it for the first time.
>
> Please:
> 1. Start with a plain-language definition.
> 2. Explain the key ideas in numbered steps.
> 3. Give one worked example.
> 4. Identify three common mistakes or misconceptions.
> 5. Finish with five short practice questions and an answer key.
>
> If the topic or course context is unclear, ask me a clarifying question. Do not invent facts; flag anything that needs checking.

## How to test
Replace `[TOPIC]` with a subject such as “confidence intervals” or “eigenvalues.” Check whether the explanation and worked example are correct, then refine the prompt if needed.

## Evaluation notes
- Was the explanation understandable?
- Was the worked example correct?
- Were the practice questions relevant?
- What should be improved?
