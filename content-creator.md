# Tech Content Creator Prompt

## Goal
Generate a concise, engaging draft for a technology-focused social media post.

## Prompt
> Act as a technology content-writing assistant. Help me draft a post about **[TECH TOPIC]** for an audience of tech learners and enthusiasts.
>
> Requirements:
> - Begin with a clear, interesting hook.
> - Explain one useful idea in accessible language.
> - Keep the draft concise and avoid unsupported claims.
> - End with one genuine question that invites discussion.
> - Provide two alternative hooks.
>
> If the topic is current or news-related, identify claims that need fact-checking instead of guessing.

## How to test
Try a topic such as “how AI assistants use prompts.” Compare the hooks and check that the final post is clear, accurate, and not exaggerated.

## Evaluation notes
- Is the hook relevant?
- Is the post understandable to beginners?
- Does the question invite a meaningful response?
